# Acme Cloud Storage - FAQ

## Plans
Acme offers three plans: Free (5 GB), Pro (1 TB, $8/month) and Team (5 TB shared, $25/month).
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Pro and Team.

## Refunds
Paid plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, refunds are
prorated for annual plans only.

## Security
All files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and required on Team.

## Support
Free users get community forum support. Pro users get email support within 24 hours.
Team users get priority chat support during business hours (9am-6pm IST).
